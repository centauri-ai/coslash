Yesterday

What this concept is arguing

The page stops being a uniform list of records and becomes a triage surface: something may be asking for you, some things are happening, everything else is history — and some things we genuinely don't know about. Visual weight follows that ranking exactly. v2 moves this to the light theme so it sits beside Concepts 1 and 2 on the same ground and the structural argument can be judged on its own, rather than winning on novelty.

V2 · LIGHT

Glass works in light, and is now comparable

Dark was a product choice, not a requirement of the material. Translucency lives in chrome, hero and live cards; text always sits on a ≥72% opaque ground. Same three-weight hierarchy, directly comparable with Concepts 1 and 2.

V2 · FROM REVIEW

Motion is opt-in

v1 animated drift, sweep and pulse unconditionally with no prefers-reduced-motion query. All three are now gated; the ambient field is static by default and the sweep degrades to a solid rule.

V2 · FROM REVIEW

"41 finished" was wrong

They are 41 Inactive. Inactive may mean interrupted, abandoned or failed — STATUSES has no completed state, and asserting one would misrepresent the data. Language corrected throughout.

V2 · FROM REVIEW

Unknown and Idle exist now

The 9 Unknown sessions get a rail entry, a stream band and their own row treatment stating that liveness is unreadable and they are not finished. Idle appears in the rail with a zero count rather than being silently absent.

V2 · FROM REVIEW

The gauge measures, it doesn't predict

v1 claimed the context gauge "predicts a compaction before it happens". It reports measured usage; compaction timing depends on what the agent does next. Relabelled "26% ctx" with a measurement-only tooltip.

V2 · FROM REVIEW

"Answer & resume" is marked speculative

It assumed an input path back into a live agent that the existing resume/handoff actions don't establish. It's now visibly marked as a future capability, with Open in Claude Code as the working route beside it.

V2 · FROM REVIEW

Honest grouping in the rail

Repositories are derived from canonical repo identity. The 26 sessions in generated folders like ge/ and wha/ sit under Ungrouped rather than being dressed up as projects.

CAUTION

Still to validate

Contrast must be checked on the rendered composite, not on token values — translucent surfaces over a gradient can fail where the tokens pass. And backdrop-filter is still limited to rail, hero and live cards; stream rows use flat translucent fills deliberately.
